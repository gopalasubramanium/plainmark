# Plainmark’s commitments

1. **Always free to use.** Official releases have no purchase requirement, subscription, license activation, or paid feature tier.
2. **No ads.** No sponsored placements, referral banners, or attention-driven notifications.
3. **No tracking.** No analytics, telemetry, fingerprinting, or automatic document uploads.
4. **No bundled extras.** Install Plainmark and the required platform runtime—nothing else. No browser extensions, system optimizers, or promotional software.
5. **Local and portable files.** Plain UTF-8 Markdown stays in folders the user chooses. No proprietary document format or lock-in.
6. **Small, understandable software.** Prefer a few dependable features. A new dependency must earn its place.
7. **Open development.** Public source, documented builds, and a GPL license that keeps distributed derivatives open under its terms.
8. **Respect the user’s time.** No account wall, onboarding funnel, upgrade prompts, or background update service.

These are commitments for the official project, not additional restrictions added to the GPL. Other people’s forks may make different choices as permitted by that license.
